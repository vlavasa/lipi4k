## A place for writing that takes its time

**Lipi4k** gives essays, notes, and personal journals room to breathe. Built with Astro and written in Markdown, it keeps *the words at the centre*.

- **Long reads** — essays and stories worth settling into.
- **Small discoveries** — notes, useful ideas, and lessons from everyday work.
- **Your own space** — a home for writing you want to keep.

Browse [the latest posts](./posts/), explore [the Markdown examples](./posts/markdown-elements/), or [read about Lipi4k](./about/).
